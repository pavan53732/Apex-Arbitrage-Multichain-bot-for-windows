# Monitoring and Observability

## Purpose
Defines health checks, telemetry, diagnostics, alerts, thresholds, dashboards, and operator-facing visibility.

## Responsibilities
- Monitor trading, execution, AI, simulation, runtime, and wallet health.
- Capture metric, log, and trace data.
- Trigger alerts from policy thresholds.

## Metrics
Latency, error rate, queue depth, fill rate, slippage, gas cost, reorg count, recovery time, and operator overrides.

## Alerting
Alerts must classify severity, owner, and action required. Safety-critical alerts must be immediate.
